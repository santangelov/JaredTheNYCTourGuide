Not a Fixed Catalog

These Tours Are Starting Points, Not Limits

Jared's private tours are built around the actual people taking them. A first-time visitor may want the essential New York landmarks. A returning visitor may want hidden history, a particular neighborhood, architecture, food, music, art or a subject that rarely appears on a standard sightseeing itinerary.

You can stay focused on one area or combine experiences into a half-day or full-day route. Central Park can lead into the Met. Lower Manhattan can combine early New York, Wall Street, the harbor and the World Trade Center. A family itinerary can balance famous sights with places that keep children or teenagers engaged.

  • Adjust the pace for your group
  • Combine neighborhoods, museums and themes
  • Spend more time on what interests you and skip what does not
  • Use limited time in New York more efficiently

Choosing Your NYC Tour

Do I have to choose one of the tours shown on this page?

No. The featured tours are useful starting points, not a complete catalog. Jared can build a private tour around a neighborhood, historical subject, museum, food interest, family priority or combination of places that matters to your group.

Can we combine several NYC destinations in one tour?

Yes. The practical combination depends on your available time, walking pace and where the places are located. Jared can help create a route that uses your time well rather than simply packing in as many stops as possible.

Are Jared's tours good for first-time visitors as well as people who already know New York?

Yes. First-time visitors can focus on the city's essential places and context, while repeat visitors can go deeper into neighborhoods, museums, architecture, history and specialized subjects they may have missed on earlier trips.

Can Jared adapt a tour for children, teenagers or older family members?

Yes. Private touring makes it possible to adjust pace, content and the amount of walking around the ages, interests and needs of the group. Share those details when you contact Jared so they can be considered in the plan.

Not Sure Which Tour to Choose?

Tell Jared when you are visiting, who is coming and what interests you. He can help turn those ideas into a private New York tour that makes sense for your group.
